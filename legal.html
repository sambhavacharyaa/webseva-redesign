Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WebSeva ("WebSeva", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit our website, purchase a reseller hosting plan, or use our services. By using WebSeva, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by your use of our services, and limited information from third parties:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, billing address, and password when you sign up.
  • Billing information: payment details processed securely through our payment providers — WebSeva does not store full card numbers on its own servers.
  • Service data: domain names, DNS records, and configuration details needed to provision and manage your reseller hosting account.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and log data collected automatically when you use our website or client area.
  • Support communications: messages, emails, and chat transcripts when you contact our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provision, operate, and maintain your reseller hosting account.
  • Process payments, renewals, and refunds.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate service updates.
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • Send you account, billing, and — where you've opted in — marketing communications.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences (such as dismissing the promo banner), and understand how visitors use our site. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect parts of the site's functionality.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Infrastructure and technology partners — including Bisup Innovations, which powers the hosting infrastructure behind WebSeva — solely to deliver your hosting service.
  • Payment processors, to complete transactions securely.
  • Service providers who help us operate our business (e.g. email delivery, support tooling), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and regular monitoring — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and billing information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with legal, accounting, and reporting obligations. You may request deletion of your data as described below.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of marketing communications at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's Privacy

WebSeva's services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, email us at contact@webseva.com.

Refund Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

We want you to be confident in choosing WebSeva. This Refund Policy explains when and how refunds are issued for our reseller hosting plans.

1. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Every new WebSeva reseller hosting plan includes a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied within your first 30 days of service, you can request a full refund of your hosting fees.

2. Eligibility

The money-back guarantee applies to your first purchase of a hosting plan. It does not cover:

  • Domain name registrations or renewals, which are non-refundable once processed.
  • Add-on services or third-party licenses purchased separately.
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for violating our Terms & Conditions or Acceptable Use Policy.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email contact@webseva.com from your account email address with your account/order details. Our team will confirm eligibility and process approved requests back to your original payment method.

4. Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 7–10 business days. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take a few additional days for the refund to appear in your account.

5. Renewals & Recurring Billing

The 30-day guarantee applies to your initial purchase only. Subsequent renewal charges are generally non-refundable, though we review renewal-related refund requests on a case-by-case basis — reach out and we're happy to take a look.

6. Chargebacks

If you have a billing concern, please contact us directly before filing a chargeback with your bank — we can typically resolve billing issues faster this way. Accounts with unresolved chargebacks may be suspended pending investigation.

7. Contact Us

Have a question about a charge or a refund? Email us at contact@webseva.com and we'll help sort it out.
